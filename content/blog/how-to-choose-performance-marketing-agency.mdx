India's performance marketing agency market has a significant information asymmetry problem. There are hundreds of agencies competing for your budget, most with identical positioning ("data-driven," "ROI-focused," "certified"). The bad ones look almost identical to the good ones on paper.

The cost of choosing the wrong agency is not just the retainer. It's months of misaligned campaigns, budget burned on the wrong objectives, attribution blind spots that make optimisation impossible, and opportunity cost while your competitors compound their paid acquisition advantage.

The eight questions below are designed to expose that difference — in a 60-minute evaluation call. Each question has a reveal: what a strong answer looks like, and what a weak answer signals.

---

## 1. What's your India-specific campaign mix — and can you show me recent India account data?

**Why this matters:** Any agency can run Google or Meta Ads. Very few understand the structural differences between running campaigns in India versus Western markets — different CPC benchmarks, payment flows (UPI, cash on delivery), festive season dynamics, and the split between English and Hindi intent signals.

An agency without recent India account data is operating on global playbooks in a market that requires local calibration.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "Our median India Google Search CPC across verticals is ₹15–₹40 for non-branded terms. Our Meta CPM for India audiences runs ₹80–₹200 depending on the audience segment and creative freshness. Here's a sanitised report from a current India client showing our cost-per-lead breakdown by city."

**What a weak answer signals:** Global benchmarks presented as India benchmarks, or vague statements like "we work with Indian businesses" without actual India performance data.

---

## 2. How do you handle attribution for the Indian customer journey?

**Why this matters:** Indian customers convert across website forms, phone calls, WhatsApp chats, offline visits, and multi-session research journeys. Standard Google and Meta pixel tracking captures a fraction of these touchpoints.

An agency that measures success only through platform-reported ROAS is optimising on incomplete data — and optimising on incomplete data produces incomplete results.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "We configure call tracking for phone leads, set up Meta Conversions API alongside the pixel, integrate WhatsApp-assisted conversion tracking for B2C clients, and implement offline conversion import for clients where sales happen outside the digital funnel. Your reported ROAS from Meta alone will always look lower than what we track in your CRM — and we reconcile that gap monthly."

**What a weak answer signals:** "We use the pixel and Google Tag Manager." That's table stakes. An agency that doesn't discuss the gap between platform-reported and true attributed revenue has not solved India attribution.

---

## 3. Show me your account structure for a client in my vertical.

**Why this matters:** How an agency structures campaigns reveals their competence more than any case study. Campaign architecture determines Quality Scores, Smart Bidding efficiency, cross-channel budget allocation, and long-term account scalability.

Common India agency mistakes: too few ad groups (poor Quality Score), overreliance on broad match (irrelevant traffic), no Performance Max separation from Search, and no India-specific negative keyword architecture.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "For [your vertical], we typically run separate brand and non-brand Search campaigns, layered with Performance Max using custom audience signals. We maintain a negative keyword library of 800–1,200 terms for India, and separate campaigns by match type and intent signal. Here's a sanitised account structure from a similar client."

**What a weak answer signals:** Generic campaign names (Campaign 1, Campaign 2), no mention of negative keywords, or Performance Max deployed without audience signal strategy. These are visible in any account audit.

---

## 4. What does your creative process look like — and who produces the creative?

**Why this matters:** [Performance marketing in India](/services/performance-marketing) increasingly rewards creative quality. After Meta's Andromeda update, creative became the primary targeting signal on social. On Google, Responsive Search Ads reward ad copy variation. Creative is no longer a nice-to-have — it's a core competency.

Many agencies claim creative capabilities but outsource to freelancers, add significant turnaround time, or produce templated creative that looks identical across clients.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "We have an in-house creative team that produces India-specific ad creative — static, video, and UGC-format. We run structured creative tests (two variables at a time, minimum statistical threshold before declaring a winner) and refresh creative on a 3–4 week rotation. Creative performance data from the previous month is in every monthly report."

**What a weak answer signals:** "We can work with your creative team" without discussing how they manage creative testing, or creative timelines of 2–3 weeks that signal outsourcing.

---

## 5. What's your north star metric — and how do you reconcile it with platform ROAS?

**Why this matters:** Platform ROAS (what Google and Meta claim your campaigns earned) routinely diverges 20–50% from what your CRM or revenue dashboard shows. Agencies that optimise for platform ROAS without reconciling it against real business outcomes are improving a number that doesn't correspond to revenue.

The best performance marketing agencies in India build a measurement framework around **Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)** — total revenue divided by total ad spend — and use platform data as a diagnostic signal, not a success metric.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "We track MER monthly alongside platform ROAS, and our clients see an average 25–35% variance between Meta-reported ROAS and verified revenue. We run periodic blackout tests to measure incrementality — pausing a channel for 48–72 hours to see true lift. This is more useful than platform attribution for budget allocation decisions."

**What a weak answer signals:** Presenting platform ROAS as the primary success metric, or not being able to explain the difference between last-click attribution and data-driven attribution.

---

## 6. Who will actually manage my account — and what's their India experience?

**Why this matters:** Many Indian agencies pitch senior strategists but deliver junior execution. The person who ran the case study in your pitch meeting may never log into your account. This is particularly common at agencies that have grown faster than their talent base.

Ask explicitly: who will run your campaigns day-to-day? What's their Google Ads or Meta Blueprint certification status? How many accounts do they manage simultaneously?

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "Your account will be managed by [Name], who has 3 years of India-specific paid media experience and manages 6 accounts currently. I'll be involved in strategy reviews quarterly. We cap account managers at 8 accounts — above that, campaign quality degrades." Names, account loads, and seniority signals.

**What a weak answer signals:** Vague answers about "a dedicated team," avoidance of account load numbers, or pivots back to agency-level credentials rather than individual account manager qualifications.

---

## 7. What are your contract terms — and who owns the account?

**Why this matters:** Some Indian agencies create ad accounts, audience lists, conversion tags, and product feeds under their own MCC (Google) or Business Manager (Meta). When you part ways, they keep the assets. Rebuilding these takes months and you lose historical performance data.

Account ownership, notice periods, and data portability should be non-negotiable.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "We operate inside your Google Ads MCC and your Meta Business Manager. You own every asset — campaigns, audiences, conversion tags, product feeds. If we ever part ways, you take a fully-structured account. Our minimum contract is 3 months; after that, 30-day notice. You keep everything."

**What a weak answer signals:** Reluctance to discuss account ownership, agency-controlled accounts presented as normal, or contracts with 6+ month minimum terms and no exit clause.

---

## 8. Walk me through your pricing model — what's included and what isn't?

**Why this matters:** Indian agency pricing is frequently opaque. Common problems: percentage-of-spend models that create perverse incentives to increase budgets; "management fees" that don't include creative; reporting that costs extra; onboarding fees that aren't disclosed upfront.

Clear pricing signals operational maturity. Unclear pricing signals that costs will escalate unpredictably.

**What a strong answer sounds like:** "Our fee is ₹[flat amount]/month. This includes campaign management, bi-weekly optimisation, monthly reporting, and up to 4 creative assets per month. Media spend is a separate line — you pay Google and Meta directly. Onboarding is included. There are no performance bonuses or hidden fees."

**What a weak answer signals:** Percentage-of-spend models (creates incentive misalignment), vague "starting from" pricing without scope clarity, or multiple add-ons required for a functional engagement.

## Red Flags to Exit the Conversation

Beyond the eight questions, certain signals should end the evaluation immediately:

- **Guarantees on ROAS or lead volume.** No agency can guarantee outcomes on platforms they don't control. Guarantees signal either dishonesty or fundamental misunderstanding of how digital advertising works.
- **Refusal to share current account structure.** Sanitised examples require no NDAs. Refusal suggests there's nothing to show.
- **Pricing significantly below market.** India [Google Ads management](/services/google-ads-management) at ₹15,000/month for a ₹3,00,000 spend account is not a deal — it's a signal of inexperienced execution.
- **No mention of testing frameworks.** Campaigns that are not systematically tested don't improve. Every credible agency has a creative testing and bid strategy testing protocol.
- **Engagement metrics as success metrics.** Likes, follows, impressions, and reach are not performance marketing metrics. An agency that leads with these is not a performance agency.

---

## Scoring Your Evaluation

Rate each of the eight questions on a 1–3 scale:
- **3**: Clear, specific answer with India data or examples
- **2**: Good principles, light on specifics
- **1**: Vague, generic, or deflected

A total score of 20+ is a strong signal. 15–19 warrants a follow-up call on weak areas. Below 15, keep looking.
